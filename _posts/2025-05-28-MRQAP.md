In my previous post, “Internal Migration—Florida Analysis,” I analyzed migration within Florida and created a network. In this post, I will continue with the MR-QAP analysis.

❓ What is **MR-QAP**

**MR-QAP (Multiple Regression Quadratic Assignment Procedure)** is a regression technique used to analyze network data. When applied to the Florida internal migration analysis I am doing, it can answer how well specific relationships between counties explain the connections observed in the actual network. Furthermore, MR-QAP does not simply perform calculations, but also performs permutation tests.
