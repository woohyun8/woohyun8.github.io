# Introduction

This project was carried out as part of CSI 500 at George Mason University Korea, under the guidance of Professor Sohyun Park. I explore the **Global Trade Flows of Maize and Poultry** through the view of network analysis(NetworkX), with a focus on building and visualizing trade networks.

This was my first time working with NetworkX and social network analysis, so I encountered many challenges and shortcomings. That said, I would like to share this project, which took approximately five weeks of steady work.

Above all, I want to thank Professor Park for her insightful feedback and encouragement throughout the project. Let’s dive in!
